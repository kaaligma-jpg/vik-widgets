What We Offer

Seven paths. One broker.

From a first purchase to a reverse mortgage in retirement, and everything a homeowner or investor needs in between — one point of contact for the whole journey.

01 / First-Time Buyer & New to Canada

A clear path in, for buyers just starting out.

Whether you're buying your first home or building credit history after moving to Canada, programs exist to help you qualify — often with a smaller down payment or alternative credit verification.

First-Time Buyer New to Canada FHSA Eligible Down Payment Support
  • First-Time Home Buyer Programs
    Government incentives and lender products designed to lower the barrier to a first purchase, including FHSA-funded down payments.
  • New to Canada Mortgage Programs
    Alternative credit verification for buyers with limited Canadian credit history. Newcomer programs typically run along three tracks: an insured track for straightforward, moderate-income files; an asset-based track for those with significant savings but non-traditional income; and a "liquid reserve" track for newcomers still establishing their career, who qualify for a larger mortgage by holding extra funds in reserve rather than relying on income alone.
  • Down Payment Planning
    Mapping out the RRSP Home Buyers' Plan, FHSA, and gifted-funds rules so your down payment is structured correctly before you apply.

02 / Private First Mortgage

The foundation loan, built around your timeline.

A private 1st mortgage replaces or originates the primary loan on a property when speed, flexibility, or income documentation rule out a bank — residential or commercial.

Self-Employed Friendly Bruised Credit OK Fast Closings Residential + Commercial
  • Residential Purchases & Refinances
    Primary financing for buyers and owners who don't fit conventional bank criteria but have solid equity or a clear exit.
  • Commercial & Mixed-Use Property
    First-position financing for retail, multi-family, and mixed-use buildings, including time-sensitive closings.
  • Construction & Bridge
    Short-term first mortgages that carry a project from purchase or construction through to permanent financing.

03 / Renewals & Refinancing

Do not just sign what the bank sends you.

At renewal, most homeowners simply sign the rate their existing lender offers, often without knowing whether it is competitive. A broker shops it for you, at no cost to you.

Renewal Shopping Refinance Rate Comparison No-Cost Review
  • Renewal Review
    Comparing your current lender's renewal offer against the wider market before you sign anything.
  • Refinancing
    Restructuring your existing mortgage to access equity, consolidate debt, or change your rate or term.
  • Switch & Transfer
    Moving your mortgage to a new lender at renewal, typically at no cost to you.

04 / A-Lender & B-Lender Financing

The right lender for your file, not just the biggest bank.

Conventional bank financing works well for straightforward files. When it does not, alternative (B) lenders fill the gap — for self-employed income, newer credit, or unique property types.

A-Lender B-Lender Self-Employed Alternative Income Verification
  • Conventional (A-Lender) Mortgages
    The lowest-rate financing for straightforward, fully-documented income and credit files, through major banks and credit unions.
  • Alternative (B-Lender) Mortgages
    Financing for self-employed borrowers, newer credit history, or income that does not fit standard bank documentation.
  • Stated Income & Bank Statement Programs
    Qualification based on bank deposits or a signed income statement, for business owners whose tax returns do not reflect full income.

05 / Home Equity Line of Credit

Flexible access to the equity you have already built.

A HELOC lets you borrow against your home's equity as needed, repaying and re-borrowing, rather than taking one fixed lump sum.

Revolving Credit Renovation Financing Debt Consolidation Flexible Access
  • Standalone HELOC
    A revolving credit line secured against your home's equity, used and repaid as needed.
  • Readvanceable Mortgage + HELOC
    A mortgage structure where your available HELOC room increases automatically as you pay down principal.
  • Common Uses
    Renovations, debt consolidation, or a flexible source of funds for other opportunities as they come up.

06 / Private Second Mortgage

Fast capital, layered behind your existing mortgage.

When banks say no or timelines are too tight, a private 2nd mortgage unlocks the equity sitting behind your current loan — for residential or commercial property.

Debt Consolidation Renovation Bridge Financing Residential + Commercial
  • Residential 2nd Mortgages
    Consolidate high-interest debt, fund renovations, or cover life expenses using existing home equity.
  • Commercial 2nd Mortgages
    Bridge working capital, expansion, or tax obligations against commercial or mixed-use property equity.
  • Flexible Qualification
    Approvals based primarily on property equity and exit strategy, not just income documentation or credit score.

07 / Reverse Mortgage

Turn home equity into retirement income.

For homeowners 55+, a reverse mortgage releases tax-free equity with no required monthly payments — so you can stay in the home you love while funding the retirement you planned for.

Tax-Free Equity No Monthly Payments 55+ Eligible Stay In Your Home
  • Access Up To 55% of Home Value
    Unlock tax-free equity based on your age, home value, and location — with no impact on OAS or GIS benefits.
  • No Required Monthly Payments
    Repay only when you sell, move, or the last borrower passes away — the loan is settled from the home's sale proceeds.
  • Guaranteed to Stay In Your Home
    You retain title and ownership, and can never owe more than the fair market value of your home at settlement.
  • Flexible Fund Use
    Use the funds for renovations, healthcare, debt payoff, gifting, travel, or simply topping up monthly income.
