What We Offer

Three doors. One purpose.

We don't chase every product on the shelf — we go deep on the three that solve most of what Ontario homeowners and property owners actually come to us for.

01 / Reverse Mortgage + LLQP

Turn home equity into retirement income.

For homeowners 55+, a reverse mortgage releases tax-free equity with no required monthly payments — paired with the full suite of LLQP-licensed insurance, savings, and income products to build the complete retirement picture.

Tax-Free Equity No Monthly Payments 55+ Eligible Full LLQP Suite
  • Reverse Mortgage Financing
    Access up to 55% of your home's value, stay in your home, and never owe more than fair market value at settlement.
  • Life Insurance
    Term, whole, and universal life coverage to protect estate value and provide for beneficiaries.
  • Critical Illness Insurance
    Lump-sum coverage paid on diagnosis of a covered illness, protecting savings and equity from health shocks.
  • Disability & Income Protection
    Replaces a portion of income if illness or injury prevents you from working.
  • Segregated Funds
    Principal-protected investment funds with maturity and death-benefit guarantees, held outside your estate.
  • Annuities
    Guaranteed income streams that convert savings into predictable, lifelong or fixed-term payments.
  • Accident & Sickness Insurance
    Coverage for medical expenses and income loss arising from accidents or illness.
  • Group & Travel Insurance
    Employee benefit plans and travel medical coverage for individuals and small businesses.

02 / Private Second Mortgage

Fast capital, layered behind your existing mortgage.

When banks say no or timelines are too tight, a private 2nd mortgage unlocks the equity sitting behind your current loan — for residential or commercial property.

Debt Consolidation Renovation Bridge Financing Residential + Commercial
  • Residential 2nd Mortgages
    Consolidate high-interest debt, fund renovations, or cover life expenses using existing home equity.
  • Commercial 2nd Mortgages
    Bridge working capital, expansion, or tax obligations against commercial or mixed-use property equity.
  • Flexible Qualification
    Approvals based primarily on property equity and exit strategy, not just income documentation or credit score.

03 / Private First Mortgage

The foundation loan, built around your timeline.

A private 1st mortgage replaces or originates the primary loan on a property when speed, flexibility, or income documentation rule out a bank — residential or commercial.

Self-Employed Friendly Bruised Credit OK Fast Closings Residential + Commercial
  • Residential Purchases & Refinances
    Primary financing for buyers and owners who don't fit conventional bank criteria but have solid equity or a clear exit.
  • Commercial & Mixed-Use Property
    First-position financing for retail, multi-family, and mixed-use buildings, including time-sensitive closings.
  • Construction & Bridge
    Short-term first mortgages that carry a project from purchase or construction through to permanent financing.
