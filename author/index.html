About the author

Vikas Sharma is a Canadian mortgage broker, team lead and author with 23 years in the industry — a bank desk in 2003, Scotiabank’s President’s Award in 2018 and 2020, and today a team of mortgage agents at Pineapple Financial that he mentors through their first 90 days. He is licensed in mortgages and in life and health insurance (LLQP), writes in English, Hindi and Punjabi, and lives in Brampton, Ontario. Canadian Mortgage Professional named him a Rising Star in 2025.

“True success in financial services comes from alignment, not accumulation. Awareness has to grow alongside income — or the income does not last.”
