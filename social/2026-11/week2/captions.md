## Post 2 — Bank said no · Fri Nov 13, 7:00 AM
File: A_post2_bank_said_no.png

Bank said no. It's a detour, not a dead end.

Today: a B-lender approves — higher rate, 1–2 year term, you get the house. Next 12–24 months: pay on time, rebuild credit, build equity, file clean taxes. At renewal: back to an A-lender, lower rate, fresh start.

On $500,000, the detour costs roughly $580 a month more than an A-lender rate — for the bridge years only. Waiting two more years to buy usually costs more.

The exit plan is written before the first payment. Otherwise it isn't a detour, it's a destination.
